# NaviGarde IA - Demo Pitch Script (2 Minutes Max)

## 0:00 - 0:30 | The Problem & Hook
*(Visual: Artisanal pirogues at Beninese docks, crowded boats)*

*"Every day, thousands of passengers board artisanal pirogues across Benin's waterways with little to no safety oversight. Overcrowding, missing life jackets, and hidden structural issues put lives at risk. Manual reporting is slow, inconsistent, and impossible to audit at scale."*

*"NaviGarde IA changes that. Built for Meta's Global AI Developer Hackathon under 'Build for Your World', it's an intelligent maritime safety infrastructure that puts AI in service of human judgment."*

## 0:30 - 1:15 | Live Demonstration
*(Visual: Switch to NaviGarde IA interface)*

*"Watch this. A field agent inspects a pirogue using their smartphone. With one photo upload, SAM 3.1 segments the vessel - detecting passengers, life jackets, motor, hull, and buoys."*

*(Visual: Show segmentation results and risk analysis)*

*"Muse Spark then reasons over those segments with the declared capacity. In seconds, it computes passenger load, life vest coverage, and flags critical violations like overcrowding or insufficient safety equipment. The system recommends an action, but never enforces it."*

*(Visual: Show report generation)*

*"From there, Muse Wonder drafts an official administrative report in the proper maritime format. If a distress call comes in, Muse Voice Transcribe converts it to structured alert data for the Capitainerie dashboard."*

## 1:15 - 2:00 | Trust, Proof & Conclusion
*(Visual: Show PDF export with hash and QR code)*

*"What sets NaviGarde IA apart is sovereign traceability. Every report is SHA-256 hashed and displayed as a digital sovereignty seal on the regulatory PDF, complete with a QR code for instant verification."*

*(Visual: Show versioning - validate then edit)*

*"Crucially, once a supervisor validates a report, it's immutable. Any corrections trigger automatic versioning - creating v2, v3 while preserving the full audit trail. The hash is recalculated on every read to detect any tampering."*

*"L'IA propose, l'humain décide. That's our principle. NaviGarde IA brings Meta's multimodal intelligence to Benin's waterways - turning smartphones into intelligent inspection tools and giving port authorities the traceability they need to save lives."*

*"Thank you."*
